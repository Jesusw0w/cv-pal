# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: everyone taking part — contributors, maintainers, people opening issues —
should be treated with respect. Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning or
discriminatory comments are not acceptable, in the repository or anywhere the project is
discussed.

This matters more than usual here. CV Pal is used by people looking for work, often
during a stressful and precarious period. Issues and discussions may involve someone's
real employment history, career gaps, or circumstances. Treat that information, and the
person attached to it, with care.

## Reporting

There is no contact address for this project, so use one of:

- **[GitHub's abuse report form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse)** — the right
  route for anything GitHub itself can act on, and it does not require the maintainer to
  see it first.
- **A [private security advisory](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/security-advisories/guidance-on-reporting-and-writing-information-about-vulnerabilities/privately-reporting-a-security-vulnerability)**
  on this repository — not what the feature is named for, but it is the one channel that
  reaches the maintainer privately rather than in public view.

Reports are handled privately and in confidence.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits, issues and contributions that do not follow
this code, and may ban anyone whose behaviour is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
